# Texting & conversation

!!! warning "Mixed — real rapport skills tangled with manipulation"
    Some of this is ordinary good conversation (curiosity, attention on the other person); some is
    deliberate manipulation (engineered push-pull, faked mistakes, manufactured emotional swings). The
    note keeps the former and flags the latter — the manipulative moves erode the trust you're trying
    to build and tend to backfire on anyone perceptive.

> **TL;DR.** The chat advice is mindset-first ("relax — it's not an audition"), then tactical: keep
> attention on the other person, mirror and build on their topics, modulate warmth. The genuinely
> useful 80% is just *be curious and present*; the rest is gameplaying.

**Provenance:** `cited` · **Confidence:** low · **Updated:** 2026-06-03

## The sound part (keep this)

- **Mindset over scripts.** Most "I can't talk to him" failures are anxiety, not a lack of lines.
  Relaxing is the actual fix. (Echoes [reappraisal vs. suppression](../eq/reappraisal-vs-suppression.md):
  manage your own state first.)
- **Put attention on them.** Restate what they said with warmth, notice something specific about them,
  ask about *their* topic instead of yanking it back to yourself. This is just **active listening** —
  the most reliable "they enjoyed talking to me" lever there is.
- **Add your own emotion.** Replace a flat "goodnight" with a little warmth. Sharing affect, not only
  information, is what makes a conversation feel alive.
- **Balance.** Don't over-talk (leave room), roughly match message length and who-initiates frequency.
  Reasonable social calibration.

## The manipulative part (flag, don't use)

- **Push-pull / 欲扬先抑** — manufacturing emotional "rollercoasters" (a staged "I'm a bit annoyed at
  you… because you're so good I'll never like anyone else") to make yourself feel exciting. This is
  **engineered intermittent reinforcement**: manipulation, transparent to perceptive people, and a
  relationship built on a gimmick.
- **Deliberate mistakes** — intentionally misreading him so he "corrects" you and feels engaged.
  Low-grade manipulation.
- **Pet names / role-play / 撒娇 (acting cute)** to fast-track intimacy — fine in moderation and when
  mutual; manipulative when used as a deliberate hook before there's real rapport.

## How to apply

- Keep the curiosity-and-presence half — it's genuine skill and it generalizes to *all* conversation,
  not just dating.
- Skip the engineered-emotion half. If a tactic only works while the other person doesn't know you're
  doing it, it's manipulation, and it ages badly.

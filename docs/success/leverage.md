# Leverage: labor, capital, code & media

> **TL;DR.** Naval's central idea: wealth comes from **leverage** — output decoupled from your hours. Three kinds: **labor** (people working for you), **capital** (money deployed at scale), and the new **permissionless** leverage of **code & media** — products with *zero marginal cost of replication* that work while you sleep, with no gatekeeper's permission.

**Provenance:** `cited` · **Confidence:** high · **Updated:** 2026-06-06

## What it is

*"You're not going to get rich renting out your time"* — linear effort can't produce non-linear wealth. Leverage is the multiplier:

- **Labor** — other people working for you. The oldest leverage; needs management, and people are reluctant followers.
- **Capital** — money you direct. Highly scalable, but **gatekept**: someone has to entrust it to you.
- **Code & media** — software, writing, video, podcasts: build once, replicate at ~zero cost to millions. **Permissionless** — no approval required — which is why it's this era's great equalizer. *"The internet allows you to scale any niche obsession."*

The grail is **stacking** them (a product built in code, funded by capital, amplified by media). Capital and labor are *permissioned*; code and media are not — so someone without a network or a balance sheet should start there.

## How to apply

- **Build leverage into your work** — ask "does this output keep earning after I stop touching it?" Prefer products, IP, and code over billable hours.
- **Start with permissionless leverage** (write, code, publish) to build a track record, *then* earn access to capital and labor.
- Stack [specific knowledge](specific-knowledge.md) (what only you can do) **×** leverage (so it scales) **×** [ownership](own-things-not-time.md) (so you keep the upside).

# Attention as value (Justin Sun)

> **TL;DR.** Sun's most distinctive — and most corrosive — model: **internet attention is "mass" that converts into economic value** (his "E=mc²" analogy), so capturing attention by almost any means is treated as value creation. It genuinely explains memecoins and virality. It also rationalizes spectacle over substance — useful to *understand*, dangerous to *adopt*.

## What it is

Justin Sun (TRON) frames **attention as convertible to value via tokens/memecoins**, the way mass converts to energy: "internet attention converts into economic value through MemeCoins." NFTs/memecoins are "better" than stored art because everyone can see them — *seen = valuable*. His stunts are consistent with the model as attention→value experiments (a $6.2M Cattelan banana he ate on camera; a $4.5M Buffett lunch).

!!! warning "Contested — describe, don't endorse"
    This is a *descriptive* model of the attention economy, **not** investing advice. It blurs into "bad fame beats no fame" and pump-by-spectacle, and it's a big part of why Sun is nicknamed "the Harvester" and drew an **SEC suit** (alleging unregistered securities and market manipulation; later reported settled). Attention with no underlying value isn't created value — it's usually a *transfer*, often from retail to insiders. Powerful for **reading** memecoins and virality; corrosive as a life or business strategy.

## How to apply (analytically)

- **Use it to read, not to run:** memecoin price can track attention, not fundamentals — model the reflexivity, don't moralize it away.
- **Treat pure-attention assets as fragile** — reflexive on the way up *and* down; don't confuse attention with a durable [moat](moats.md).
- **As a builder:** attention is a real input, but pair it with something that retains value when the attention leaves.

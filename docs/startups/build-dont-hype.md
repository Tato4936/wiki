# Build, don't hype (CZ)

> **TL;DR.** CZ's operator ethos: *"show me a live product demo, not a deck."* Build something visibly better and let it speak — "hype may win short term; builders win long term." The counterweight to marketing-first crypto. The honest caveat: build-first ≠ build-*only* — a real edge still has to be distributed.

**Provenance:** `cited` · **Confidence:** medium · **Updated:** 2026-06-08

*Distilled second-hand from a community-compiled "CZ thinking" file; quotes attributed to his tweets and memoir, not independently verified.*

## What it is

**BUIDL > TALK.** CZ's read on early Binance: the product was simply faster and cleaner than incumbents (Poloniex, Bittrex), and *that* — not marketing — won the market. "A lot of people want to walk me through a deck instead of doing a live product demo 😂." "Hype may win short term; builders win long term."

!!! note "The tension with distribution — hold both"
    This sits directly against [Distribution & sales](distribution-and-sales.md) (Thiel: a superior product is *not enough* — you must sell it). CZ concedes Binance also had very strong marketing, and that "technical excellence and business success are correlated but not identical." So the synthesis is: **build a real edge *and* distribute it** — just never mistake the deck for the product. Build-first is a bias against vaporware, not a licence to ignore go-to-market.

## How to apply

- **Diligence on what's shipped:** live product, real users, real volume — not roadmap or whitepaper.
- **As a builder:** get to a working demo fast and let product quality carry the pitch — *then* solve distribution.
- **Treat "let's walk you through our deck" as a yellow flag** when there's no demo behind it.

# Skin

Clear, healthy skin is one of the most reliable [attractiveness and health cues](../what-is-attractive.md)
— and the evidence-based version is refreshingly short. Most "skincare" is marketing; this is the part that
actually works.

## Notes

- [Skincare basics (the minimalist routine)](skincare-basics.md) ✅ — cleanse, moisturize, SPF (+ a retinoid)
- [Sunscreen](sunscreen.md) ✅ — the single highest-impact anti-aging habit (RCT-backed)
- [Retinoids](retinoids.md) ✅ — the one active worth adding, started low and slow

## The whole routine, in one line

**Cleanse · moisturize · SPF every morning · a retinoid at night.** That's 90% of the results. Add serums
only if you enjoy it, one at a time; see a dermatologist for anything persistent.

*Backlog: acne (causes & OTC actives), niacinamide / vitamin C / exfoliating acids (the optional add-ons),
hair & scalp health.*

# Body composition

> **TL;DR.** Getting to a **lean, reasonably-muscled** body composition is one of the biggest controllable
> changes to how you look — it reshapes your face and frame, makes clothes fit, and signals health. It's
> mostly **diet (body fat) + resistance training (muscle)**, and it's available to almost everyone.

**Provenance:** `cited` · **Confidence:** high · **Updated:** 2026-06-04

## What it is

Two dials:

- **Body fat %** — lower body fat reveals facial structure (jawline, cheekbones), defines the body, and
  reads as healthy/fit. This is overwhelmingly a **diet** outcome (a modest, sustainable calorie deficit) —
  you can't out-train a bad diet. This often does more for "facial aesthetics" than any forum hack.
- **Muscle** — resistance training adds shape (shoulders, back, arms), improves posture and presence, and
  fills out clothing. See the [gym section](../gym/index.md).

Together they're the difference between "the same person" looking soft/shapeless vs. lean/built — without
changing a single fixed feature. It's why body composition sits near the top of
[controllable vs. fixed](controllable-vs-fixed.md).

## How to apply

- For *facial* and *frame* gains, prioritize **getting lean** (sustainable deficit, high protein) — it's
  the change people notice most.
- Pair it with **resistance training** for shape and health — see
  [the gym section](../gym/index.md) for how to actually train and eat for it.
- Play the long game: slow, sustainable change beats crash dieting (which costs muscle and rebounds).

# The unsexy basics

> **TL;DR.** Sleep, hydration, posture, and teeth aren't glamorous, but they show up *on your face and
> frame* constantly — and they're free. Chronic poor sleep and slouching undo a lot of grooming; fixing
> them is low-effort, high-return.

**Provenance:** `cited` · **Confidence:** high · **Updated:** 2026-06-04

## What it is

The background variables that quietly set your baseline:

- **Sleep** — poor sleep visibly degrades appearance: one well-known study had observers rate
  sleep-deprived people as **less attractive and less healthy** from photos alone (eyes, skin, color).
  It's also upstream of skin, body composition, and mood.
- **Posture** — standing/sitting tall instantly changes how your body reads (and links to
  [open body language](../body-language/open-body-language.md) and confidence). It's free and immediate.
- **Teeth & smile** — clean, reasonably white teeth and a real [Duchenne smile](../body-language/the-genuine-smile.md)
  are core warmth/health signals; whitening and (if relevant) alignment are worth it.
- **Hydration & basic diet** — show up in skin and energy.

None of this is exciting, which is exactly why people skip it — and why it's available upside.

## How to apply

- **Fix sleep first** — it compounds into skin, body comp, mood, and the face. Consistent schedule, enough
  hours.
- **Posture is a free instant win** — stand and sit tall; it changes how you're read today.
- Sort **teeth** (cleaning, whitening, alignment if needed) — high warmth ROI.

# How to get funnier

> **TL;DR.** Humour is **trainable** — comedians are made, and structured humour programs measurably raise it. Fastest levers: take **improv**, **consume comedy deliberately**, be a **generous audience**, and **practice low-stakes** (banter, callbacks). It's mostly reps + the right inputs, not innate "funny."

**Provenance:** `cited` · **Confidence:** medium · **Updated:** 2026-06-06

## What it is

The trait feels fixed but isn't: humour production and appreciation both respond to training (e.g., McGhee's **7 Humor Habits** program improved humour and coping in trials), and every comedian started unfunny. The inputs that move the needle:

- **Improv** — the single highest-ROI move. *"Yes, and"* (accept + build), reacting bigger, and *making your partner look good* train spontaneity and play directly — **and a class is itself a recurring, social, friend-making hobby.**
- **Deliberate input** — watch/read a *lot* of comedy you genuinely find funny (standup, sitcoms, witty writers). You absorb timing, structure, and references the way you'd build any [specific knowledge](../success/specific-knowledge.md).
- **Be a generous audience** — practising easy, genuine laughter ([appreciation is half the skill](shared-laughter-and-bonding.md)) lowers the self-monitoring that blocks most people.
- **Low-stakes reps** — light [teasing](../connection/affectionate-teasing.md) and callbacks with safe people; notice what lands and why (see [the craft](the-craft.md)).

!!! note "The real unlock for most people"
    It's usually not technique — it's **permission to be playful** and to stop self-censoring. Lower the bar, commit to the bit, let yourself be a little silly. Trying to be *clever* is exactly what reads as try-hard.

## How to apply

- **Sign up for an improv class** — technique and a social hobby in one.
- **Build a comedy "diet"** of things you actually find funny, and pay attention to *why* each bit works.
- **Commit and stop hedging** — a half-said joke dies; say it like you mean it, then move on fast if it misses.

# Connection & rapport

Applied, interpersonal techniques for the thing the rest of the wiki mostly *defines*: **being liked,
building rapport, and bringing relationships closer.** Where [Emotional Intelligence](../eq/index.md)
explains *what* emotional skill is, this section is the *how-to* — concrete moves drawn from negotiation
(Voss), relationship science (Gottman, Gable, Aron), and the classics (Carnegie).

Each note is one technique: what it is, why it works, and exactly how to do it. Status flags: ✅
well-supported · ⚠️ mixed / limited evidence.

## Map

### Make people feel heard
- [Labeling ("It seems like…")](labeling.md) ✅ — name their emotion out loud to defuse and connect
- [Mirroring](mirroring.md) ✅ — repeat their last few words to keep them talking
- [Validation](validation.md) ✅ — make their reaction feel legitimate, even without agreeing
- [Ask follow-up questions](ask-follow-up-questions.md) ✅ — the simplest, best-tested way to be liked

### Make people open up
- [Calibrated questions](calibrated-questions.md) ✅ — open "How/What" questions that hand them control
- [The 36 questions](the-36-questions.md) ✅ — escalating mutual disclosure that builds closeness fast
- [The vulnerability loop](vulnerability-loop.md) ✅ — go first; trust is built by matched risk

### Make people feel good around you
- [Active-constructive responding](active-constructive-responding.md) ✅ — how you react to their *good* news
- [Lead with warmth](lead-with-warmth.md) ✅ — people read warmth before competence; earn trust first
- [Genuine interest (Carnegie)](genuine-interest-carnegie.md) ✅ — the durable core of *How to Win Friends*
- [Bids for connection](bids-for-connection.md) ✅ — turn *toward* the small invitations people make
- [Specific compliments](specific-compliments.md) ✅⚠️ — make praise specific, and give far more of it

### The quieter forces — liking under the hood
- [Similarity & mere exposure](similarity-and-mere-exposure.md) ✅ — we like the similar and the familiar
- [Cialdini's liking principle](cialdinis-liking-principle.md) ✅ — what drives liking, as a persuasion lever
- [The Franklin effect](the-franklin-effect.md) ⚠️ — asking a small favor can increase liking
- [The pratfall effect](the-pratfall-effect.md) ⚠️ — a humanizing blunder can make the competent more likable

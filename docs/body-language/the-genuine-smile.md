# The genuine (Duchenne) smile

> **TL;DR.** A real smile involves the eyes, not just the mouth — the crinkle from the muscle around the
> eye (the "Duchenne marker"). People read eye-crinkle smiles as warmer and more trustworthy, and smiles
> are contagious, so a genuine one is a fast, free warmth signal.

**Provenance:** `cited` · **Confidence:** high · **Updated:** 2026-06-04

## What it is

Ekman and colleagues distinguished the **Duchenne smile** (genuine enjoyment — *zygomaticus major* pulling
the mouth **plus** *orbicularis oculi* crinkling the eyes) from a **social "say-cheese" smile** (mouth
only). Observers reliably rate Duchenne smiles as more genuine, likable, and trustworthy, and — via
[emotional contagion](../eq/emotional-contagion.md) — a real smile tends to pull one back from others.

The eye-crinkle is harder (though not impossible) to produce on command, which is part of why it reads as
authentic.

## How to apply

- The shortcut to a warmer smile is to **actually summon the feeling** — recall something you genuinely
  like about this person or moment — and let the eyes engage, not just the mouth. A mouth-only smile can
  read as polite but cold.
- Smiles are **contagious and disarming**: a genuine one early lowers others' guard and sets a warm tone —
  a quick [warmth](../connection/lead-with-warmth.md) signal.

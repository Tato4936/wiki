# Healthspan vs. lifespan

> **TL;DR.** The goal isn't just *more years* (lifespan) but more *good* years — **healthspan**, the time you stay physically and cognitively functional. Attia's frame: most premature death comes from **four chronic diseases**, and the way to a strong final decade is to define the physical life you want at 90 and **reverse-engineer the training now.** You're building the substrate decades ahead of needing it.

**Provenance:** `cited` · **Confidence:** medium · **Updated:** 2026-06-08

## What it is

Attia (*Outlive*) argues medicine is good at acute problems but weak at slow chronic decline. His **"Medicine 3.0"** is proactive and prevention-first, organized around the **four horsemen** that cause the large majority of non-accidental death:

1. **Atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease**
2. **Cancer**
3. **Neurodegenerative disease** (e.g., Alzheimer's)
4. **Metabolic dysfunction / type-2 diabetes** — often the upstream amplifier of the other three.

The actionable centerpiece is the **"Centenarian Decathlon"**: name the ~10 physical things you want to still do in your last decade (carry groceries up stairs, lift a grandchild, hike), then train *now* for the much higher capacity that requires — because function declines from whatever peak you built. The substrate is unglamorous: [aerobic fitness](zone-2-and-vo2max.md), strength, stability, and [metabolic health](nutrition-fundamentals.md).

!!! warning "Practitioner frame — specifics are opinion"
    The *strategy* (optimize healthspan; train ahead; fitness & metabolic health are load-bearing) is sound and mainstream-compatible. But *Outlive* is one physician's synthesis, and some specifics — supplement stacks, exact targets, certain protocols — are **opinion or contested**, not settled science. Take the frame; scrutinize the details.

## How to apply

- **Define your Centenarian Decathlon** — the concrete capacities you want at 85–90 — and let it set today's training targets.
- **Train the substrate:** [Zone 2 + VO₂max](zone-2-and-vo2max.md), [strength](../gym/index.md), and stability/balance (the anti-fall insurance).
- **Defend metabolic health early** — it's upstream of three of the four horsemen.

# Zone 2 & VO₂max

> **TL;DR.** **Cardiorespiratory fitness is among the strongest predictors of how long you live** — low fitness carried risk comparable to or worse than smoking or diabetes, with no observed ceiling to the benefit. The practical build: a large base of easy **Zone 2** cardio plus a little hard **VO₂max** interval work. This is the most evidence-backed longevity lever you can actively train.

**Provenance:** `cited` · **Confidence:** high (the mortality link) / medium (protocol specifics) · **Updated:** 2026-06-08

## What it is

In a retrospective cohort of **~122,000 patients** who did treadmill testing, **Mandsager et al. (2018)** found higher measured cardiorespiratory fitness strongly predicted lower all-cause mortality — benefits continued into the elite-fitness range (no ceiling), and being in the **lowest-fitness group carried risk on par with or exceeding** that of smoking, diabetes, or established heart disease. Few modifiable factors show an association this large.

Attia (*Outlive*) operationalizes it as two trainable qualities:

- **Zone 2** — sustained, conversational-pace aerobic work that builds mitochondrial density and fat-oxidation; the broad *base*.
- **VO₂max** — the ceiling of aerobic capacity, raised by short, hard intervals; a top-tier predictor in the data above.

⚠️ The mortality *association* is robust (and partly reverse-causation — illness lowers fitness); the specific **doses/protocols** below are reasonable practitioner guidance, not precise prescriptions.

## How to apply

- **Most cardio easy:** ~**150–180 min/week of Zone 2** (can talk, not sing) — the unglamorous base.
- **Add ~1 weekly VO₂max session:** hard intervals (e.g., 4×4 min near-max, 4 min easy between).
- **It complements lifting,** not replaces it → [the gym](../gym/index.md); together they cover the healthspan substrate.

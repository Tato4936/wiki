# The two-engine plan

> **TL;DR.** Two engines drive this. **Engine 1:** compound an *owned* capital base at a strong, sustainable long-run return — enough on its own to reach the lower targets over ~20–30 years. **Engine 2:** convert a verifiable track record into *external* AUM + carry + GP ownership — the lever that reaches the top target faster and more reliably than compounding alone.

## What it is

The setup: access to a capital base that **scales with performance** (a smaller allocation now, growing as the track record compounds), a **fundamental-investing network and mentorship** to tap, and a **quant/AI research stack** of your own. The plan turns that into two compounding engines.

**Engine 1 — compound the owned base.** Grow the capital at a high, *sustainable* rate. Treat the scaling allocation as a **performance ladder**: returns + low drawdowns unlock the next tranche. Permanent capital has a practical ceiling; the job is to compound it for decades (see [the math](the-compounding-math.md)).

**Engine 2 — scale beyond the base.** The bigger lever is *other people's money*. A clean, audited multi-year track record + your [quantamental edge](the-quantamental-edge.md) + a credible network = the story to raise external AUM. Carry on a large fund plus ownership of the management company is how managers reach the top tier *personally* far faster than compounding a fixed base.

### Phases
- **Phase 0 — now (training):** build the research engine, the credential, and the network; define the process.
- **Phase 1 — early base (~7 yrs):** prove returns, *institutionalize a verifiable track record*, fuse systematic + fundamental into one repeatable process.
- **Phase 2 — scale + structure:** formalize a fund / family-office (risk systems, ops, team); open to external LPs.
- **Phase 3 — compound + scale (20–30 yr):** Engine 1 compounds the base; Engine 2 grows AUM and carry toward the top target.

## How to apply

- **Optimize for track record, not just returns, early.** Auditable, low-drawdown numbers are the asset that unlocks both the scaling allocation *and* external capital.
- **Settle the ownership structure up front:** what capital is *yours to own* vs. capital you *manage*? Your net worth depends on ownership (see [honest odds](honest-odds-and-risks.md)).
- **Build Engine 2 from day one** — every early decision should also pass "would this survive LP due diligence?"
